1. What CaseCheck is

CaseCheck reads the status of immigration court cases from the public EOIR automated case information system, shows it in a readable form, and tells you when it changes. That is the whole service.

CaseCheck is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. Nothing on this site is a substitute for an attorney or an accredited representative. Decisions about your case — what to file, when to appear, how to respond — belong with a lawyer.

2. The information we show

We show what EOIR publishes for the case number you give us. We do not create, correct, or influence that record. If the government system is down, slow, or returns something unexpected, our result will reflect that.

Court schedules change, and systems have delays. Never rely on CaseCheck alone for a hearing date. Confirm through the official EOIR hotline at 1-800-898-7180, through the court, or through your attorney. Missing a hearing can end a case — treat our alerts as a helpful signal, not proof.
