1. What CaseCheck is

CaseCheck reads the status of U.S. immigration cases, shows it in a readable form, and tells you when it changes. For immigration court cases it reads the public EOIR automated case information system. For USCIS cases it reads the USCIS Case Status API using the receipt number you give us. That is the whole service.

CaseCheck is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. Nothing on this site is a substitute for an attorney or an accredited representative. Decisions about your case, such as what to file, when to appear, or how to respond, belong with a lawyer.

CaseCheck is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Department of Justice, EOIR, USCIS, or any government agency.

2. The information we show

We show what the government publishes for the case number you give us. We do not create, correct, or influence that record. If a government system is down, slow, or returns something unexpected, our result will reflect that.

Court schedules change, and systems have delays. Never rely on CaseCheck alone for a hearing date or a deadline. Confirm through the official EOIR hotline at 1-800-898-7180, the USCIS Contact Center at 1-800-375-5283, the court, or your attorney. Missing a hearing can end a case. Treat our alerts as a helpful signal, not proof.
