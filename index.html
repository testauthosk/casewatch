FAQ

Questions, answered.

The most common ones are here — the rest are on the full FAQ page.

Where does the status come from?

Directly from the official EOIR automated case information system — the same source as the government's 1-800 hotline and website, just easier to read and watched for you around the clock.

What do I need to check my case?

Just your 9-digit A-Number and your country of citizenship. No documents, no account for your first check.

How will I be alerted when something changes?

By email and/or Telegram — your choice. The moment a hearing date, court, or decision changes, you'll know.

Is my information private?

Yes. We only use your case number to look up your status. We don't ask for documents and never sell your data.

Can I use it in Spanish or Russian?

Yes — CaseCheck works in English, Spanish and Russian, on the web and in the Telegram bot.
